# CS-139 — Derive `has_developer_direct_hint` from `contract_type` for F5 benchmarking

## Context

After CS-138 wired `F2Orchestrator` → `economics.analyze()`, `has_developer_direct_hint` was still hard-coded to `False`. That pushed every CVC/CVP/APV contract through the `BANK_PURCHASE` rate band, contradicting [[PRD_F5_ANALISIS_ECONOMICO]] BR-10 for CVP contracts ("For a CVP contract directly with developer, the benchmark is the direct-developer range, not the bank range"). The §8.1 classifier prompt (`backend/classification/application/prompts.py:58-60`) defines CVP as:

> "Compraventa de inmueble a plazos (precio pagadero en cuotas con o sin intereses, **directo con vendedor o desarrollador, sin que sea financiamiento bancario regulado**)."

So the classifier itself guarantees that any contract emerging as CVP excludes bank financing. The hint is derivable from `contract_type` alone — no prompt change, no LLM retraining, no new boolean in `ElementsDetected`.

## Scope

**In:**

- `F2Orchestrator._build_economic_summary` derives `has_developer_direct_hint = (aggregated.contract_type is ContractType.CVP)` and passes it to `economics.analyze(...)`.
- The CVP happy-path orchestrator test asserts that the persisted envelope's rate comparison is anchored on the developer-direct band (`metric_label = "Tasa efectiva anual vs financiamiento directo"`).

**Out (explicit):**

- Extending the hint to APV / LEA / CVC: their financing arrangement is not fixed by contract type alone. Promoting any of them would require a clause-level financier signal (e.g. detect mention of a regulated bank as financier). Tracked as a future EPIC-04 follow-up if it becomes load-bearing for B5 evaluators.
- Adding a `developer_direct_financing` boolean to `ElementsDetected`: unnecessary given the deterministic derivation above, and would force coordinated changes in the §US-05 prompt + EPIC-06 evaluators per the `ElementsDetected` docstring contract.
- LEA: routed through the bank band today. RUBRICA §14 lists LEA in `developer_direct_rate_*` `applicable_contract_types`, but the bank band is the conservative default until product confirms whether LEA's third-party financier qualifies as "developer-direct".

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `F2Orchestrator._build_economic_summary` passes `has_developer_direct_hint=True` whenever `aggregated.contract_type is ContractType.CVP` and `False` otherwise. _Implementation at `backend/classification/application/orchestrator.py:504-528`._
- [x] CVP happy path persists an `EconomicSummary` whose first rate-band `benchmark_comparison` carries `metric_label = "Tasa efectiva anual vs financiamiento directo"`. _Pinned by `test_happy_cvp_persists_contract_analysis` in `backend/tests/test_f2_orchestrator.py`._
- [x] CVC / APV / FSV / LEA paths are unaffected (FSV keeps its own band; APV/CVC/LEA keep BANK_PURCHASE). _No regressions in the existing economics + orchestrator suites (111 passed)._
- [x] Derivation is deterministic and LLM-free (PRD_F5 BR-13). _One-line ternary on `contract_type`; no new LLM call sites._
